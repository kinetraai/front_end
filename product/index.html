Engineering Innovation for a Connected Floor

Engineering Better Motion for Robotics. In an environment where technology evolves at a dizzying pace, Kinetra has become a fundamental pillar to protect engineering assumptions and operational interests.

Kinetra is an AI-native Motion, Robotics & Kinematics platform designed to accelerate the development, simulation, and deployment of intelligent robotic systems across industrial, mobile, and humanoid domains.

Traditional CPU-based motion planners take seconds to generate collision-free trajectories, making real-time reactive control impossible for high-degree-of-freedom (DoF) robots. Kinetra unifies GPU-accelerated kinematics solving, collision-free motion generation, robot learning, and edge deployment to achieve sub-100ms planning.
