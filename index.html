Why Kinetra Exists

Our Journey in Modern Robotics

Built because robotics and manufacturing teams were managing robot geometry, motion constraints, and simulation evidence across disconnected tools, spreadsheets, and messages — making current status, ownership, history, and required next steps hard to pin down.

We bring the key parts of robotics engineering into one connected workspace, from robot models and motion studies to simulations, reviews, and project results. This makes it easier for teams to find past work, stay updated, share information, and make confident decisions without switching between different tools. We're focused on helping robotics teams manage their daily engineering work in a simpler and more organized way.

Frequently Asked Questions

Understand Your Robotics Workflow

Kinetra is an engineering platform for robotics teams to manage robot models, validate designs, and coordinate engineering workflows.

It provides a centralized library for storing, organizing, versioning, and accessing robot models and related engineering data.

It provides a structured approval process where reviewers can comment on issues, link physical-test evidence, and formally approve exports before they are used in production.

Kinetra keeps models, validation records, review comments, and approval history in one shared workspace, helping engineering teams coordinate their work.

It tracks model versions and changes throughout the engineering workflow, helping teams maintain a clear record of revisions and approved configurations.
